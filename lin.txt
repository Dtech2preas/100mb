Here's a complete step-by-step guide.

Step 1: Back up your files

Before changing partitions, copy any important files to an external drive or cloud storage.


---

Step 2: Download Linux Mint

On Windows, open your browser and go to:

[Linux Mint Download Page](https://linuxmint.com/download.php?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Download the Cinnamon Edition (64-bit) ISO.


---

Step 3: Get Rufus

Go to:

[Rufus](https://rufus.ie?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Download the latest version.


---

Step 4: Create a bootable USB

1. Insert your 8 GB (or larger) USB flash drive.


2. Open Rufus.


3. Under Device, select your USB.


4. Click SELECT and choose the Linux Mint ISO you downloaded.


5. Leave the other settings at their defaults (unless Rufus suggests changing them).


6. Click START.


7. Wait until it says READY.




---

Step 5: Make space for Linux

1. Press Windows + X.


2. Click Disk Management.


3. Right-click C:.


4. Click Shrink Volume.


5. Enter 100000 MB (about 100 GB) or 150000 MB (about 150 GB).


6. Click Shrink.



You'll now see Unallocated space. Do not create a new partition there.


---

Step 6: Boot from the USB

1. Leave the USB plugged in.


2. Restart the laptop.


3. As soon as it starts, repeatedly press Esc.


4. An HP Startup Menu should appear.


5. Press F9 for Boot Device Options.


6. Select your USB drive and press Enter.




---

Step 7: Try Linux

When Linux Mint starts, choose Start Linux Mint (or similar). It will run from the USB without changing your hard drive.

If everything works (Wi-Fi, keyboard, touchpad, etc.), double-click Install Linux Mint on the desktop.


---

Step 8: Install Linux

1. Choose your language.


2. Choose your keyboard layout.


3. Connect to Wi-Fi if prompted.


4. Tick Install multimedia codecs.



When you reach Installation type:

If you see Install Linux Mint alongside Windows Boot Manager, choose that. This is the easiest option.

If you don't see it, let me know before continuing.



---

Step 9: Finish the installation

1. Choose your time zone.


2. Create your username and password.


3. Click Install.


4. Wait 10–30 minutes.


5. When it's finished, click Restart Now.


6. Remove the USB when asked.




---

Step 10: Choose your operating system

Each time you turn on your laptop, you'll see a menu where you can choose:

Linux Mint

Windows 10
